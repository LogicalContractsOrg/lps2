# Supervisory Recordkeeping & Disclosure

The IOSCO Supervisory Toolkit's recordkeeping, audit-trail and disclosure expectations, expressed as computable policies over an AI system's decision/execution trace.

The 11 policies of the Ponens pack `iosco-supervisory-recordkeeping`, as a program of Logical English for LPS: each policy is a law that records a breach, and each of the 2 traces under `sources/traces/` is a scenario. Choose a scenario and press **Run**: the breaches are on the timeline, at the step where each policy failed, and the comment above each scenario says what Ponens's own evaluator found on the same trace.

Ledger: 11 policies encoded, 0 kept as residue (formulas Ponens's own parser cannot read).

The pack is Imandra's reading of: IOSCO FR/02/2026 — Supervisory Toolkit for AI Use in Capital Markets (May 2026).
