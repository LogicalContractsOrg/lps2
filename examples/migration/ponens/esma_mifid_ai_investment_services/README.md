# MiFID II AI in Investment Services

ESMA's MiFID II expectations for AI in retail investment services — best interest, suitability, transparency, accuracy controls, testing, and recordkeeping — as computable policies.

The 14 policies of the Ponens pack `esma-mifid-ai-investment-services`, as a program of Logical English for LPS: each policy is a law that records a breach, and each of the 2 traces under `sources/traces/` is a scenario. Choose a scenario and press **Run**: the breaches are on the timeline, at the step where each policy failed, and the comment above each scenario says what Ponens's own evaluator found on the same trace.

Ledger: 14 policies encoded, 0 kept as residue (formulas Ponens's own parser cannot read).

The pack is Imandra's reading of: ESMA Public Statement ESMA35-335435667-5924 (30 May 2024).
