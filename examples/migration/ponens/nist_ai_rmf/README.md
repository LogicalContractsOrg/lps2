# NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The NIST AI RMF 1.0 — GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE and the seven trustworthiness characteristics — as computable policies over an AI system's lifecycle record.

The 15 policies of the Ponens pack `nist-ai-rmf`, as a program of Logical English for LPS: each policy is a law that records a breach, and each of the 2 traces under `sources/traces/` is a scenario. Choose a scenario and press **Run**: the breaches are on the timeline, at the step where each policy failed, and the comment above each scenario says what Ponens's own evaluator found on the same trace.

Ledger: 15 policies encoded, 0 kept as residue (formulas Ponens's own parser cannot read).

The pack restates: NIST AI 100-1, AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (2023).
