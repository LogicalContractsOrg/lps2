translator program (LE2's migration/drools, over LPS2's src/syntax/lps_drools.pl). It is a changed form of

its scenario restate FireExample.java of the same module. Fire.wording and fire_alarm.drools.json are not
from Apache KIE: the first was written for this repository, the second is the record of a run of Fire.drl
on Drools 10.2.0.
